# Boneyard Bounce — Game Design Document

## High concept
A spooky-cute, single-screen physics puzzle. Gravekeeper Hazel Grimm fires a limited queue of silver, fire, or heavy pellets from Betsy, using ricochets and environmental reactions to clear each room.

## Player promise
Read the room, plan a bank shot, and enjoy a compact chain reaction. The graveyard is playful and eerie, with no blood or realistic violence. Ghouls vanish in mint ectoplasm puffs and skeletons scatter harmlessly cartoon bones.

## Core loop
Inspect the room and hint → aim by dragging backward or pointing → preview the first travel segment and bounce → fire a pellet → let physics settle → win with all hostiles removed or retry after spending the queue.

## Progression
Five themed chapters, ten rooms per chapter, gradually introducing crates, barrels, mirrors, wisps, portals, magnets and moving hazards. Three stars reward efficient ammo use; a hidden gold skull marks an optional objective.

## Controls
Mouse/touch drag or point to aim and release to fire. Keyboard/gamepad aim and Space/A fire. R restarts and Escape pauses. Controls and aim assist are configurable.

## Presentation
Moonlit purple/teal with moon-yellow and orange accents, expressive thick outlines, layered graveyard art, gentle hit-stop, screen shake, squash-and-stretch, and a quiet spooky waltz under clear sound effects.
