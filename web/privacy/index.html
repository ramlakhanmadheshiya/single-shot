One Clean Shot

Privacy

Effective 10 October 2026

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, no analytics. We don't collect your data.
  • Your progress, stars and settings stay on your device.
  • The Android app shows a small banner and optional reward videos from Google AdMob. Google may collect some data to show ads; where the law requires it, you're asked for consent first.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your card details.
  • The web version shows no ads and has no purchases.

Who we are

One Clean Shot is a minimalist ricochet puzzle for Android and the web, made by Ramlakhan Madheshiya (theramlakhan.com). In this policy, "we" means the developer. Questions? Email ramlakhanmadheshiya7@gmail.com.

What's stored on your device

The game saves a small file with:

  • which rooms you've cleared and your stars,
  • your settings (sound, music, theme, orb style and similar),
  • your daily hint count and when it was last refilled,
  • which purchases are unlocked, and any tips you've given.

On Android this lives in the app's private storage; on the web it lives in your browser's storage for this site. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else. We have no server that stores player data, no account system and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Ads (Android only)

The Android app uses Google AdMob to show a small banner at the bottom of the screen and, only if you choose to watch one, a rewarded video that gives you a hint or a 6-hour try of a cosmetic pack. There are no full-screen interstitial ads.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and how you interact with ads. Google handles this data under its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

Your consent

Where required (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before any personalised ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time with the Ad privacy choices button in Settings.

Reset or limit your advertising ID

On most Android phones open Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads). From there you can reset your advertising ID or delete it to opt out of personalised ads across apps. Menu names vary a little between phone makers.

Removing ads

The Supporter pack or Complete pack removes the banner. The web version never shows ads.

Purchases (Google Play)

Optional one-time purchases (no subscriptions) are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment details; we never see your card number or billing address. The app only receives confirmation of which item you bought, and stores that unlock on your device. Because purchases are linked to your Google account, you can get them back with Restore purchases in the shop. Google's handling of payment data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing

When you tap share, the game creates an image of your result and opens your device's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you pick an app and send it yourself. What happens next is up to the app you share with.

Children

One Clean Shot is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you're a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we'll help.

Deleting your data

Since your data lives only on your device, you're in full control:

  • Reset progress in the game's Settings clears your rooms and stars.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android Settings, deletes everything it saved.
  • On the web, clearing this site's data in your browser deletes your progress.

Purchases remain tied to your Google account and can be restored after reinstalling. For data held by Google (ads or payments), use your Google Account settings.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we'll update this page and the effective date at the top.
