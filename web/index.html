Chapters

Nine ideas, in order.

Each chapter adds one new rule to the bank shot, and each has its own colour and mood.

  1. Dawn

    Plain walls. Learn the bank shot.

  2. Glass

    Panes break after one bounce.

  3. Gate

    Hit the switch to dissolve the gates.

  4. Drift

    Moving platforms. Time your release.

  5. Turn

    Pivot walls turn 90° when hit.

  6. Shift

    Portals send the orb somewhere else.

  7. Void

    Dark blocks swallow; bumpers bounce.

  8. Prism

    Prisms split the orb. Ringed targets.

  9. Spark

    Flying through a spark adds a bounce.

Every chapter opens with a tutorial room. Bounces climb from 1 to 8 across each chapter. Every room is proven solvable in one shot by a solver.

How it plays

Aim. Release. Clear.

  1. Drag to aim

    The dots above the launcher show how many bounces this room allows.

  2. Release

    The orb flies and banks off the walls — and whatever else the chapter adds.

  3. Pop every target

    All of them, in that one shot. A miss resets the room; try as often as you like.
